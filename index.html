Where the brain's white matter meets its function.

An interactive companion to Human white matter tracts aligned with canonical functional brain networks. Explore the cortical endpoints of 51 major tracts, the cognitive topics decoded from them, and how they align with the brain's canonical functional networks.

How to explore

Three steps, from the whole brain to a single topic.

The explorer is organized as a drill-down. Start broad with the clusters, pick a tract, then dig into the cognitive topics and networks behind it. Every panel is drawn live from the data — nothing is a static figure.

STEP 01

Pick a cluster

Tracts are grouped by how similar their functional profiles are (hierarchical clustering on the decoding scores). Each cluster shows its combined endpoint map, top topics and network overlap.

Open cluster view → STEP 02

Choose a tract

One tract at a time: its 3D cortical endpoint map in both orientations, and the decoding of the joint map next to each endpoint on its own.

Open tract view → STEP 03

Dig into a topic

Click any topic bar to see its full 40-term list with LDA weights, switch between functional and all terms, and compare with the radar chart, word cloud and network correspondence.

What's inside

Everything in the paper, live in the browser.

3D cortical endpoint maps

Per-tract and per-cluster endpoint density on the fsaverage surface, rendered from the GIFTI files. Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom.

Topic decoding

Pearson correlation between each endpoint map and 104 NeuroQuery LDA topics, computed with the gradec toolbox (Peraza et al., 2024).

Radar charts & word clouds

The paper's figures, regenerated live for any tract or cluster, with a switch between functional-only and all terms.

Network correspondence

Dice overlap and spin-test significance against six canonical network atlases, via the Network Correspondence Toolbox (Kong et al., 2025).

Full tract × topic matrix

All 51 tracts against all 104 topics as one heatmap, ordered by the dendrogram so similar tracts sit together. Click a row to open that tract.

Endpoint thresholds

Switch between the endpoint-density thresholds used in the paper (0.05–0.20) and see how the clustering solution changes.

Methods in brief

White matter tracts were segmented with the Bullock et al. (2022) taxonomy on brainlife.io.

Tract endpoints were projected to the cortical surface and decoded against NeuroQuery LDA topics with gradec.

Tracts were clustered by their decoding profiles (complete linkage on 1 − r), and endpoint maps compared with canonical networks using the Network Correspondence Toolbox.
